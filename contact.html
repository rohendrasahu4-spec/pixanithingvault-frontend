Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?
We typically respond within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, feel free to reach out via phone or WhatsApp.
Can I request a custom wedding design?
Absolutely! We specialize in custom wedding designs. Select "Custom Design Request" from the subject dropdown and provide your requirements. We'll get back to you with a quote.
Do you offer refunds on digital products?
Due to the digital nature of our products, we generally don't offer refunds. However, if you face technical issues with your download, please contact support and we'll help resolve it.
Can I collaborate with Pixanithing?
Yes! We're always open to collaborations with designers, videographers, and content creators. Select "Collaboration / Partnership" from the subject dropdown to get started.
